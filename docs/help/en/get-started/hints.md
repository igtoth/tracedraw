# The Hints panel

The [[docker.hints]] panel explains the tool you are using, while you use
it. It is open at first, beside [[docker.properties]] and [[docker.objects]];
open it again with [[menu.help > menu.help.hints]] or from
[[menu.window > menu.window.dockers]].

![The Hints panel](../../img/get-started-hints.png)

## What the panel shows {#pages}

- The **home** page introduces the panel and lists topics such as drawing
  lines, shapes, selecting, moving and rotating objects, effects, outlines,
  fills, text, AI and getting help. Each topic is a link.
- A **topic** page explains the topic and lists the tools that serve it, each
  with its icon, its name and what it does. Click a tool to see its page.
- A **tool** page tells you how to use the tool, one instruction per line
  with the keys in bold, and links to the topic that lists the tool.
- An **object** page appears when you select something: it names what you
  picked (a curve, a rectangle, text, a bitmap, a group, a clip frame, a
  blend, a dimension and so on, or several objects), says what you can do
  with it, lists the tools that work on it and tells what the property bar
  offers for it.

Every page ends with [[hint.learn_more]], a link to the page of this Help
that explains the tool or topic in full.

## Moving between pages {#navigation}

When you choose another tool, the panel shows that tool's page. The bar at
the bottom of the panel has [[hint.home]] on the left and [[hint.back]] and
forward on the right, like a web browser.

Selecting an object shows its page; clicking an empty spot, so that nothing
is selected, brings back the page of the tool you are using. While you draw
with a drawing tool, the object you have just drawn keeps the tool's page in
view; click the object to see its page.
